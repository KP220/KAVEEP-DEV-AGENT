# Purpose

This document defines engineering ownership and responsibility throughout the KAVEEP ecosystem.

Every engineering activity shall have a clearly identified owner.

Responsibilities shall never be ambiguous.

Ownership shall remain traceable.

---

# Responsibility Philosophy

Authority defines who may decide.

Responsibility defines who must deliver.

Accountability defines who answers for the result.

Every engineering activity shall define all three.

---

# Responsibility Levels

## Engineering Director

Responsible for:

- Engineering vision
- Engineering strategy
- Engineering coordination
- Capability orchestration
- Cross-repository engineering

Accountable for overall engineering quality.

---

## Capability Owner

Each Engineering Capability has one owner.

Responsible for:

- Capability evolution
- Engineering quality
- Capability roadmap
- Capability documentation
- Capability standards

---

## Engine Owner

Each Engineering Engine has one owner.

Responsible for:

- Engine implementation
- Engine maintenance
- Engine testing
- Engine performance
- Engine documentation

---

## Task Owner

Every engineering task shall identify:

- Responsible executor
- Required inputs
- Expected outputs
- Completion criteria

No engineering task shall be ownerless.

---

## Report Owner

Every engineering report shall identify:

- Author
- Reviewer
- Approval authority

Reports preserve engineering accountability.

---

# Responsibility Matrix

Every engineering artifact shall define:

Owner

Reviewer

Approver

Consumers

Dependencies

Related Specifications

Related Architecture

Related Policies

---

# Responsibility Rules

Engineering responsibility cannot be delegated without explicit approval.

Engineering ownership shall always remain identifiable.

Engineering accountability shall survive personnel or AI model changes.

---

# Human and AI Collaboration

Responsibilities may be fulfilled by:

- Human Engineer
- AI Engineering Agent
- Human + AI Collaboration

The engineering process remains identical regardless of execution model.

---

# Guiding Principle

Clear ownership creates accountable engineering.

Accountable engineering creates trusted software.
