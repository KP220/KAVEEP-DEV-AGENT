# Purpose

This document defines the official Engineering Lifecycle of the KAVEEP ecosystem.

The lifecycle establishes the mandatory engineering stages that every official KAVEEP software entity shall follow throughout its existence.

It provides a repeatable, traceable, auditable, and continuously improving engineering process shared across the entire ecosystem.

No official KAVEEP software entity shall bypass this lifecycle.

---

# Engineering Lifecycle Philosophy

Software is not born when code is written.

Software is born through engineering.

Implementation is only one stage within a much larger engineering lifecycle.

Every official KAVEEP entity begins as an idea and evolves through disciplined engineering until it becomes a trusted member of the ecosystem.

# Lifecycle Stages

## 1. Idea

A new concept, problem, opportunity, or improvement is identified.

Deliverables

- Initial proposal
- Problem statement
- Expected value

---

## 2. Vision

The long-term objective of the entity is defined.

Deliverables

- Vision document

---

## 3. Mission

The operational purpose is established.

Deliverables

- Mission document

---

## 4. Identity

The entity receives an official identity within the ecosystem.

Deliverables

- Identity document
- Classification
- Responsibilities
- Authority

---

## 5. Roadmap

The long-term evolution plan is defined.

Deliverables

- Roadmap
- Milestones
- Phases

---

## 6. Architecture

The structural design is established.

Deliverables

- Architecture
- Component model
- Interfaces
- Trust boundaries

---

## 7. Specification

Functional and non-functional requirements are defined.

Deliverables

- Specifications
- Schemas
- Acceptance criteria

---

## 8. Policy Alignment

Engineering verifies compliance with KAVEEP-POLICY.

Deliverables

- Policy validation
- Risk assessment

---

## 9. Engineering Design

Detailed engineering decisions are prepared.

Deliverables

- Design decisions
- ADRs
- Engineering plans

---

## 10. Implementation

Specifications are realized through software.

Deliverables

- Source code
- Configuration
- Build artifacts

---

## 11. Validation

Engineering verifies implementation correctness.

Deliverables

- Validation reports
- Traceability records

---

## 12. Testing

Engineering confirms expected behavior.

Deliverables

- Test reports
- Coverage reports
- Test evidence

---

## 13. Documentation

Engineering knowledge is preserved.

Deliverables

- Documentation
- User guides
- Developer guides

---

## 14. Audit

Engineering history becomes immutable.

Deliverables

- Audit records
- Engineering reports

---

## 15. Human Approval

Authorized humans approve protected actions.

Deliverables

- Approval records

---

## 16. Release

Software becomes available.

Deliverables

- Release package
- Release notes
- Version

---

## 17. Operation

Software performs its intended responsibilities.

Deliverables

- Operational metrics
- Monitoring data

---

## 18. Observation

Engineering observes behavior in production.

Deliverables

- Telemetry
- Incident reports
- User feedback

---

## 19. Continuous Improvement

Engineering improves software while preserving identity.

Deliverables

- Improvement proposals
- Engineering backlog

---

## 20. Evolution

The software continues evolving without losing its original mission or architectural integrity.

Deliverables

- Updated roadmap
- Architecture evolution
- New lifecycle iteration

---

# Lifecycle Gates

No engineering stage may advance unless required deliverables have been completed.

Each stage concludes with an Engineering Gate.

Engineering Gates verify:

- Completeness
- Quality
- Traceability
- Policy Compliance
- Architectural Consistency
- Risk
- Approval Requirements

---

# Lifecycle Traceability

Every lifecycle stage shall reference its predecessor.

Every engineering artifact shall identify:

- Parent artifact
- Source authority
- Responsible engineer
- Version
- Approval status

No orphan engineering artifacts are permitted.

---

# Lifecycle Invariants

The following rules shall always remain true.

• Every entity follows the lifecycle.

• Engineering precedes implementation.

• Policy precedes execution.

• Validation precedes trust.

• Human approval precedes protected actions.

• Continuous improvement preserves identity.

# Guiding Principle

Every KAVEEP software entity shall be engineered through the complete lifecycle before it is trusted.

Trusted software is not created by implementation alone.

Trusted software is created through disciplined engineering.
