# Purpose

This document defines the official engineering vocabulary of the KAVEEP ecosystem.

Every official repository, specification, schema, document, AI agent, and software component shall use these terms consistently.

A shared vocabulary reduces ambiguity, improves interoperability, and establishes a common engineering language across the entire ecosystem.

---

# Core Engineering Terms

## Vision

The long-term aspiration describing the future state the system intends to achieve.

---

## Mission

The primary purpose and responsibility of a system.

---

## Identity

The official definition of what a system is, including its role, authority, and lifecycle.

---

## Constitution

The highest governing principles of the KAVEEP ecosystem.

No repository may contradict the Constitution.

---

## Roadmap

The strategic direction and long-term evolution plan for the ecosystem.

---

## Policy

The mandatory governance rules that define what is permitted, restricted, or prohibited.

---

## Engineering Charter

The official engineering governance document defining engineering responsibilities, ethics, quality expectations, and engineering culture.

---

## Engineering Philosophy

The engineering beliefs and principles that guide software development.

---

## Architecture

The structural design defining components, relationships, responsibilities, and constraints.

---

## Specification

A precise definition describing the expected behavior, requirements, interfaces, constraints, and acceptance criteria of a component.

---

## Implementation

The realization of a specification through software.

---

## Validation

The process of verifying that an implementation satisfies its specification.

---

## Verification

The process of confirming that evidence supports engineering conclusions.

---

## Audit

The permanent engineering record preserving traceability, accountability, and historical continuity.

---

## Traceability

The ability to trace every engineering artifact to its authoritative source.

---

## Repository

An official engineering unit within the KAVEEP ecosystem.

Repositories possess identity, history, responsibilities, and lifecycle.

---

## Engineering Artifact

Any engineering output including documentation, architecture, specifications, schemas, code, reports, tests, and audit records.

---

## Engineering Lifecycle

The complete lifecycle through which every engineering artifact evolves from idea to continuous improvement.

---

## Engineering Authority

The officially assigned responsibility to define, approve, or govern engineering decisions.

Authority is determined by the KAVEEP Engineering Authority Hierarchy.

---

## Human Approval

Explicit authorization granted by an authorized human before protected engineering actions may proceed.

---

## Engineering Foundry

The engineering environment where every official KAVEEP software entity is designed, specified, implemented, validated, documented, and prepared for release.

KAVEEP-DEV-AGENT is the official Engineering Foundry of the KAVEEP ecosystem.

---

## AI Software Engineering Organization

A governed engineering organization composed of specialized AI-assisted engineering capabilities, engines, workflows, reports, validation gates, audit records, and human approval.

An AI Software Engineering Organization is not equivalent to a chatbot or coding assistant.

---

## Thai Command Interface

The primary human communication interface through which users may express engineering intent in natural Thai.

The interface translates Thai engineering intent into governed engineering requests without weakening traceability, policy compliance, validation, or approval requirements.

---

## KAVEEP Brain

The replaceable reasoning layer used by KAVEEP-DEV-AGENT to perform engineering analysis, planning, review, and task support.

KAVEEP Brain may use cloud or local language models through an LLM Adapter.

KAVEEP engineering authority shall never depend on a single model provider.

---

## KCP Verification

The verification process that confirms engineering outputs against verified KAVEEP knowledge before approval or release.

---

# Terminology Rule

Official KAVEEP engineering documents shall use terminology defined in this glossary.

Alternative terminology should be avoided unless formally approved.

---

# Guiding Statement

Shared engineering language creates shared engineering understanding.

Shared understanding creates trustworthy engineering.
