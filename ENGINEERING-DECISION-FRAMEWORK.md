# Purpose

This document defines how engineering decisions are made within the KAVEEP ecosystem.

It establishes a repeatable, explainable, evidence-based decision framework for Human Engineers and AI Engineering Agents.

No important engineering decision shall rely solely on intuition, assumptions, or AI-generated recommendations.

Every engineering decision must be justified through this framework.

---

# Engineering Decision Hierarchy

Every engineering decision shall follow the official authority hierarchy.

```text
Constitution

↓

Roadmap

↓

Policy

↓

Engineering Charter

↓

Engineering Philosophy

↓

Architecture

↓

Specifications

↓

Implementation
```

Lower-level engineering decisions shall never contradict higher-level authority.

Every engineering decision follows:

```text

↓

↓

↓

Architecture Analysis

↓

↓

Evidence Collection

↓

Risk Assessment

↓

Alternative Evaluation

↓

Engineering Recommendation

↓

Validation

↓

↓

```

Engineering decisions are made only after sufficient evidence has been collected.

---

# Engineering Decision Questions

Every significant engineering decision shall answer:

1. Does this align with the KAVEEP Constitution?

2. Does this support the official Roadmap?

3. Does it comply with KAVEEP-POLICY?

4. Does it respect the Engineering Charter?

5. Is it consistent with the Engineering Philosophy?

6. Does it preserve the Architecture?

7. Is there an approved Specification?

8. Is sufficient evidence available?

9. What is the engineering risk?

10. Is human approval required?

If any mandatory question cannot be answered, implementation shall not proceed.

# Decision Invariants

The following rules shall always remain true.

• Decisions follow authority hierarchy.

• Evidence precedes conclusions.

• Validation precedes trust.

• Human approval precedes protected actions.

• Every decision remains explainable.
